Counts: 10 published tracks; 5 rejected pilot candidates. The compact first composite was
recovered in a second tuning pass by restoring its short straight range; its four earlier
rejections remain explicitly marked tuning_pass=1. Earlier development probes: 45 requests,
16 valid preparations (not published), 29 rejected. Including development: 60 preparations,
26 valid (10 published), 34 rejected. Failed geometry was never published as accepted.

Parameter changes (opt-in only):
- Hairpin radii 3-6 m, 180-degree left/right switchbacks, counts 5/9/15; two outer
  supporting quarter-turns and a wider first fold provide clearance around the return.
- Sweeper radii 10-14.5 m, signed 55-110-degree bends, counts 5/9 with straight gaps.
- Chicanes: radius 2.8-4 m, opposing 0.8-1.05 rad turns and 0.25 m separation.
- S-curves: initial radius 4.5-6.5 m, opposing 0.95-1.35 rad turns; second radius
  independently differs by a 1.02-1.18 ratio. Eight target windows each.
- Composites: unequal corner/turn inventories, concave turns, 2.3-3.8 m technical
  corners, short fixed straights and asymmetric closure. No repeated halfturn blocks.
- Specialist exposure floors reduced to 50%; primary active-window floor remains 50%.
  Composite primary-coverage floor 40%. Geometric safety limits are unchanged.

Representative originals versus pilot (same axes; lengths are shown, not all matched):
- Hairpin: 209 m, two 29.34-30.00 m-radius ends -> 210 m, nine 3.14-6.00 m U-turns;
  p95 curvature 0.034 -> 0.320 /m. Exposure falls 77.2% -> 58.1%, frequency rises
  0.96 -> 4.29 hairpins/100 m. This is the clearest improvement.
- Sweeper: 182 m, six 24.14-33.12 m bends -> 170 m, five 11.96-13.53 m bends;
  p95 curvature 0.041 -> 0.084 /m, with separate sustained corners and a concavity.
- Chicane: five 12.10-17.16 m-radius windows at 177 m -> eight 3.01-3.55 m windows
  at 110 m. S-curve: four 11.09-15.08 m windows at 156 m -> eight 4.55-6.47 m
  windows at 165 m. Family radii improve more clearly than whole-lap p95, which
  also includes tight supporting bends.
- Composites show distinct tight/flowing sections and concave ordering. They are not
  uniformly more severe: representative original p95 0.413 /m versus pilot 0.272-0.339.

Limitations: hairpins still share a folded-lane template; chicane/S-curve scaffolds remain
ring-like; sweeper specialization and composite severity improve unevenly. The 245 m
composite has 59% supporting geometry and 26% active curvature. Widths are uniformly
1.5 m. This is a visual geometry pilot, not a new screened independent research dataset.
Clearance is sampled, physical drivability unassessed, difficulty unrated. No simulator,
controller, training, full suite, profiling, formal release changes, commit or push.
Stopped after this pilot.
